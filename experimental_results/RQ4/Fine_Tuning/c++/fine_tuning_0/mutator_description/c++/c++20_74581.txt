Mutation Operator: Template Constraint Modification

Target: Templates with constraints

Procedure: Identify any template that has a constraint specified with the requires keyword. Within the constraint expression, replace any occurrence of a trait or type transformation operation with another operation of similar nature. For example, replace std::decay_t<T> with std::remove_reference<T>, or replace std::is_constructible_v with std::is_default_constructible_v. Ensure the replacement maintains syntactic validity but alters the semantic meaning of the constraint to potentially introduce an ambiguity or contradiction.
