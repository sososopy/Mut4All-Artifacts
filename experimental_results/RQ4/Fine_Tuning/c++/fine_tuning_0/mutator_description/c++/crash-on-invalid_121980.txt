Mutation Operator: Misplace Semicolon in Template and Concept Declaration  
Target: Templates and Concepts  
Mutation Rule:  
1. Identify a concept or template declaration in the code.  
2. In the declaration statement, insert a semicolon at the end of the line before the complete statement is written.  
3. Ensure the semicolon disrupts the declaration, such as placing it after the keyword `concept` or `template` but before the full declaration is complete.  
Example:  
Original: `template <typename T> struct S {};`  
Mutated: `template ;<typename T> struct S {};`
