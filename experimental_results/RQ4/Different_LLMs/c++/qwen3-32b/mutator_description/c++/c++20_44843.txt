Mutation operator: Introduce nested template specialization layers on existing sender types by wrapping them in multiple chained adaptors. Applies to any sender expression used in asynchronous operations. For a sender variable S in the seed program, replace its use with a chain of at least three nested adaptors (e.g., adapt(S) | adapt2 | adapt3). Each adaptor must be a template that introduces new template parameters. If the seed program lacks existing adaptors, declare three new adaptor templates (Adapt1, Adapt2, Adapt3) with template-template parameters and apply them in sequence. No new types beyond the declared adaptors are introduced.
