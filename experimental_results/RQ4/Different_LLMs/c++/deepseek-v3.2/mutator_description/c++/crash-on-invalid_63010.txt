This applies to any range-based for loop that uses a ternary operator or other conditional expression as the initializer for the loop variable, but the syntax is malformed such that the colon separating the range-init and the range expression is misplaced or omitted. The mutation operator modifies the structure of a range-based for loop by altering the placement of the colon in the loop's initialization clause, potentially creating syntactically invalid but grammatically ambiguous constructs that may trigger parser assertion failures.
