Mutation Operator: In a `#[cfg(...)]` attribute containing a `target(...)` clause with a valid key-value pair, replace the key-value pairs with two arbitrary expressions (variables, literals, or constants already present in the program), structured as `target(X, Y)` where X and Y are expressions. Example: Change `#[cfg(target(os = "linux"))]` to `#[cfg(target(F, G))]`, where `F` and `G` are existing variables or constants in the program. This mutation forces the compiler to process a `target` clause with non-key-value syntax, triggering potential ICEs in attribute parsing logic.
