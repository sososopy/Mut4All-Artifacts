Mutation operator: Replace a generic bound on an async trait method with a dependent associated type from the same trait. Apply to methods with `async fn` signatures in traits that define an associated type. For example, change `async fn handle<T>()` to `async fn handle<T: MyTrait<Assoc = <Self as MyTrait>::Assoc>>()` where `MyTrait` is the trait name and `Assoc` is its associated type. This forces the compiler to resolve a recursive binder reference, increasing likelihood of hitting ICE related to unresolved type constraints in async contexts.
