Mutation Operator: Change Yield Expression Target
Apply to: Generator yield expressions
Description: Replace the target of a yield expression with a different type or value, such as replacing a primitive type with a more complex type or introducing a new variable.
Example: Change yield 5 to yield Some(5) or yield (5, 6)
Note: This mutation operator targets the yield expression specifically and applies a change to its target, which could help expose bugs related to coroutines and generator functionality in the Rust compiler.
